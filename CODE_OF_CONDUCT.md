# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. This page summarises it; the
full text is what applies.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act in ways that
contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be empathetic and kind; respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit
  permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

The code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you
represent the project elsewhere. Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer
(@muraliseelam on GitHub), for example through the private channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
do not follow this code, and may follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) according to the impact of the behaviour.
